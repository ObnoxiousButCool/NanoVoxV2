## Transcript

Agent Michael: Choice Administrators member services, this is Michael.
Member: Hello. Gloria Mbeki, member ID CHM-9917034. I went to the emergency room in July for chest pain — it turned out to be
nothing serious, thank God. But I just received a bill for $4,200 and I don't understand it. I thought I had insurance.
Agent Michael: You do have insurance, Ms. Mbeki. Let me pull up your plan and look at that claim. Okay — you're enrolled in the
CalChoice High Deductible Health Plan. Your annual deductible is $6,000, and you haven't met any of it this year. That means claims
apply to the deductible before the plan begins paying.
Member: Six thousand dollars? I have never heard that number before in my life.
Agent Michael: It's the defining feature of the HDHP — a lower monthly premium in exchange for a much higher deductible.
Member: Nobody explained that to me. My broker, Marcus Trent, put me on this plan. He said it was the best value and the premium
was lower than what I was paying before. He never said the words six thousand dollars.
Agent Michael: Did he go through a plan comparison with you at enrollment?
Member: He showed me the premiums side by side. That's what I remember. He said 'this one saves you eighty dollars a month.'
That's what I based my decision on.
Agent Michael: I understand. And I want to be direct with you — an $80 monthly premium saving is $960 a year, against a deductible
that is $5,000 higher than a standard plan. For most members, that trade only makes sense if you rarely use care and can absorb a large
bill. That should have been explained.
Member: I'm fifty-seven years old. I have high blood pressure. I use care.
Agent Michael: Then this plan may not have been a suitable recommendation for your situation. I want to escalate this. Plan suitability at
point of sale is a broker conduct matter and I don't think this should just sit as a billing question.
Member: What happens to my bill in the meantime?
Agent Michael: The $4,200 is a valid charge under the plan terms, so I can't void it. What I can do is set up a payment plan so it isn't due
at once, and escalate the suitability question to broker relations and compliance.
Member: And if they find he did something wrong?
Agent Michael: Remedies vary — I can't promise a specific outcome. But the case gets reviewed properly, and if there's a pattern with
this broker it becomes visible.
Member: Please do it. I don't want another person my age put on this plan the same way.
Agent Michael: Escalating now. Case number BRK-2026-00518. And I'm setting up a 12-month payment plan on the $4,200 so nothing
goes to collections.
Member: Thank you.
