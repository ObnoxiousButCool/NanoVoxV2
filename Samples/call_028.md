## Transcript

Agent Danielle: Thank you for calling Choice Administrators. This is Danielle.
Member: Hi Danielle, Diana Park, CHM-5561903. I want to change my primary care doctor. I've been with my current PCP for about a
year and the wait times are terrible — I can never get a same-day or next-day appointment. By the time I get in, whatever I had is
resolved or gotten worse. Is there a process to switch, and can I do it anytime or only during open enrollment?
Agent Danielle: Diana, changing your PCP on an HMO plan can be done at any time — you are not locked in until open enrollment. You
can request a PCP change effective the first of any month. Let me walk you through how.
Member: Oh, I didn't realize I could change anytime. I've been putting up with this for a year thinking I had to wait.
Agent Danielle: A very common misconception. The easiest way is through your member portal — log into choicemember.com, go to
Plan & Coverage, and select Change Primary Care Physician. You can search by location, language, and accepting new patients. The
change takes effect the first of the following month if submitted before the 15th, or the first of the month after that if submitted after the
15th.
Member: Can you help me find some options now? I'm in the Pasadena area.
Agent Danielle: Sure. In the 91101 zip code, I can see several highly-rated in-network PCPs accepting new patients. Two strong
options: Dr. Lisa Chang at Pasadena Medical Associates — patients report short wait times and same-day sick appointments are
available. Also Dr. Eduardo Morales at Pacific Primary Care — his office uses an online scheduling system which often has more
availability than calling in.
Member: I like the idea of online scheduling. That would solve a lot of my frustration right there. How do I know if Dr. Morales is
accepting new patients?
Agent Danielle: I can see in our system that Dr. Morales is currently marked as accepting new patients. I'd still recommend calling his
office directly to confirm, because that status can change.
Member: What happens to my referrals and any pending things with my current PCP?
Agent Danielle: Any active referrals from your current PCP remain valid — a PCP change doesn't invalidate existing referrals. If you
have any ongoing conditions your current PCP manages, I'd recommend scheduling a final visit or requesting your medical records be
transferred to the new PCP. You can request records directly from your current PCP's office.
Member: That's really helpful. So I can change effective October 1st if I submit before September 15th?
Agent Danielle: Exactly right. Do you want me to initiate the change right now on your account or would you prefer to do it through the
portal?
Member: I'll do it through the portal — I want to spend some time choosing. But thank you for explaining it so clearly. I wish I had
called a year ago.
Agent Danielle: Now you know. Is there anything else I can help with?
Member: No, that's everything. Thank you, Danielle.
