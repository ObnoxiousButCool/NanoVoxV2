Agent Danielle: Choice Administrators member services, this is Danielle.
Member: Hi, Angela Kim, CHM-6671039. I'm calling because I went in for my annual physical last month and I got a bill from my
doctor's office for a $35 copay. My understanding was that annual preventive physicals are free under the ACA. Why am I being
charged?
Agent Danielle: Angela, you're correct that preventive annual exams are covered at 100% with no member cost-sharing when they're
billed as preventive care by an in-network provider. Let me look at how this claim was processed. Give me just a moment.
Member: Sure. And I want to be clear — I specifically went in for my annual physical. I didn't bring up any new problems. I didn't ask
for anything extra.
Agent Danielle: I have the claim. I can see your visit was on July 8th with Dr. Rodriguez at Westside Family Medicine. The claim was
processed, but I can see that in addition to the preventive exam code, your doctor's office also billed a separate problem-focused
evaluation code. When an office bills both a preventive and a problem-oriented service in the same visit, the problem-oriented portion is
subject to your regular copay.
Member: I didn't ask to address any problems. I just went in for my physical. Can a doctor add a problem code without telling you?
Agent Danielle: Technically providers can bill for any services rendered, but you're not obligated to pay for a problem-focused service
you didn't request or that wasn't discussed. This happens sometimes when doctors document a finding they observed and bill it as a
separate service. The right step here is to first contact Dr. Rodriguez's billing office and tell them you came in only for a preventive visit
and did not request any problem-focused evaluation — and ask them to review and potentially recode the claim.
Member: And if they say they're standing by the charge?
Agent Danielle: Then you can file a billing dispute with us. I'll flag your account so that if a dispute claim comes in for that July 8th visit, it
goes to our claims review team for a reprocessing review. If the problem-focused code was not associated with a service you requested
or received, we can request a corrected claim from the provider.
Member: So I should start with the doctor's office, and if that doesn't work, come back to you?
Agent Danielle: Exactly. And when you call the billing office, use the phrase 'I'm requesting a review of the CPT codes billed for my July
8th visit — I came in for a preventive annual exam only and did not request a problem-focused evaluation.' That specific language helps
them understand what you're disputing.
Member: That's very specific and very helpful. Thank you. I wasn't sure if I was just wrong about the rules.
Agent Danielle: You weren't wrong. Preventive care is supposed to be free. Is there anything else I can help with?
Member: No, that's perfect. Thanks, Danielle.
