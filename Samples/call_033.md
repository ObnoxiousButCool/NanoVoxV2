Agent Carlos: Your doctor needs to write a 90-day prescription with refills — most maintenance medications allow this. Once you have

Member: Can I do it for both medications at the same time?
Agent Carlos: Yes — you can enroll both in the same request. One doctor visit or a call to your doctor's office to convert your

Member: Is there any reason I should keep going to the retail pharmacy instead?
Agent Carlos: For ongoing maintenance medications like yours, mail-order is almost always better. The only scenario where retail is
better is if you need a medication urgently and can't wait 7-10 days, or for a short-term medication like an antibiotic where you only need
10 days. For daily chronic medications you take indefinitely, mail-order is the better choice.
