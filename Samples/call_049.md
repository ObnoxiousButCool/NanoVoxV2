September for 2 years. I'm going to have coverage through my new employer there, so I need to cancel my CalChoice plan. What's
the process?
Agent James: Peter, congratulations on the opportunity! Let me help you cancel cleanly. When is your last day in the US and your start
date in Germany?
Member: I leave September 14th. I start officially October 1st in Germany.
Agent James: For a voluntary termination, you can request an effective date. Would you like your CalChoice coverage to end
September 30th, so you have domestic coverage through the end of September while you're still in the US and transitioning?
Member: That actually makes a lot of sense. Yes, September 30th.
Agent James: I'm noting the voluntary termination request with an effective date of September 30th. You'll receive a confirmation letter
within 5 to 7 business days. Your final premium will be for September, and any automatic payments or payroll deductions should stop
after September.
Member: Should I do anything about my prescriptions before I go?
Agent James: Great question. If you have any ongoing prescriptions, check whether your German employer's health plan covers the
same medications — German formularies are different from US formularies. It might be worth getting a 90-day supply of critical
medications through your US plan before you go, especially if you're on any specialty drugs, to give yourself time to navigate the German
system.
Member: I take a generic blood pressure medication. Should be fine in Germany but good point.
Agent James: For a generic like that, you'll likely be fine. One more thing — you'll want to keep a copy of your CalChoice EOB history
and claims records from this year. If you need to reference past treatments or medications with a new provider in Germany, having that
history is useful.
Member: How do I download that?
Agent James: Log into choicemember.com, go to Claims History, and you can export a PDF of all your claims for the current plan year.
I'd do that before your coverage ends. You can also download your member documents from the same portal.
Member: Perfect. Is there anything else I need to do?
Agent James: That's everything. Your cancellation is logged, effective September 30th. You're all set. Safe travels, Peter.
Member: Thank you, James. Very easy process.
