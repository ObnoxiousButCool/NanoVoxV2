Agent Priya: Thank you for calling Choice Administrators, this is Priya.
Member: Hello. My name is Walter Nkemelu, member CHM-3319940. I received a letter that my dialysis center is leaving your
network on October 1st. I have end-stage renal disease. I go three times a week. I cannot just find somewhere else — the scheduling,
the transport, my nurses know my access site. Please tell me what happens to me.
Agent Priya: Mr. Nkemelu, I want to start by telling you that you have strong legal protections here, and we are going to work through
this on this call. End-stage renal disease with ongoing dialysis is exactly the situation continuity of care law is written for. Let me pull up
your account.
Member: Thank you. I have been worried since the letter came.
Agent Priya: I understand. Okay, I can see your account and I can see your dialysis claims — three sessions weekly at Bayview Renal
Center, consistent since 2023. And I can see the network termination effective October 1st.
Member: So it is true.
Agent Priya: The termination is real, but it does not have to mean you lose your center. Under California continuity of care provisions, a
member receiving ongoing treatment for a serious chronic condition can continue with their existing provider at in-network cost-sharing
after that provider leaves the network. Dialysis for ESRD is a clear qualifying condition.
Member: How long can I continue?
Agent Priya: For chronic conditions requiring ongoing treatment, continuity of care can extend up to twelve months, and it can be
renewed if the clinical need continues. For dialysis specifically, extended and renewed authorization is common because there is no
natural end point to the treatment.
Member: What do I need to do?
Agent Priya: A continuity of care request needs to be submitted. Normally the provider or your nephrologist initiates it. But given the
October 1st date and the seriousness of your condition, I am not going to leave that to chance. I am escalating this to our member
advocacy team today with a clinical advocate assigned, and that advocate will contact Bayview Renal Center directly to initiate the
request rather than waiting for them to act.
Member: You will contact them for me?
Agent Priya: Yes. You should not have to coordinate this while managing dialysis three times a week. Your advocate will call you within
24 hours to confirm, and they will stay with this case until the authorization is approved.
Member: I did not expect this. I expected to be told to find a new center.
Agent Priya: No. Your access site, your care team, your schedule — those are clinical continuity factors, not conveniences. Disrupting
them carries real risk. Your case number is COC-2026-00733. Please write that down and give it to Bayview if they contact you.
Member: COC-2026-00733. Let me write it again. Yes, I have it.
Agent Priya: One more thing. I am also flagging that this network termination affects other dialysis patients. Our advocacy team will
identify every ESRD member at Bayview and reach out proactively rather than waiting for them to call frightened, the way you did.
Member: That is good. There are others there like me. Some do not speak English well. They would not know to call.
Agent Priya: Then I am glad you did, because you may have just helped them too. Your advocate will call within 24 hours, Mr. Nkemelu.
Member: Thank you. Truly.
