Member: What do other parents do?
Agent Michael: Three common approaches. One, the university student health center — most universities have one and the fees are

enrollment to a plan with a national network.
Member: Telehealth works out of state?
Agent Michael: Yes. Telehealth providers on your plan can treat her in Arizona. For something like strep, a telehealth visit and a
prescription sent to a local pharmacy would be covered at your normal $25 copay.
Member: That solves most of it, honestly.
Agent Michael: It covers a lot of routine needs. Between the student health center, telehealth, and emergency coverage, most situations
are handled. The gap is non-emergency in-person care beyond what student health provides.
Member: And at open enrollment I should look at a national network plan?
Agent Michael: Worth comparing, yes. The Plus EPO has broader out-of-area coverage.
Member: Okay. That's very helpful, thank you.
