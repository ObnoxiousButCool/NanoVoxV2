Agent Sarah: With a change in household income, you may qualify for a special enrollment period even outside open enrollment. A job
loss for any household member is a qualifying event. Covered California can assess your eligibility immediately online. If you qualify for
Medi-Cal, you could transition to that coverage — which would be zero premium — and maintain your healthcare.
Member: This is giving me a real option I didn't know I had. Even if we get back on our feet in a month, knowing there's a path that
isn't just 'pay or lose coverage' helps.
Agent Sarah: That's exactly why I wanted to tell you. Your 31-day grace window gives you time to explore this. I'm noting on your

Agent Sarah: Always. Take care, Maria.
