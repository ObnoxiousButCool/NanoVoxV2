problem Background

Their member call center handles several thousand calls a month. Agents are speaking with enrolled members about plan issues, billing disputes, claims, provider questions — and Choice has zero insight into what's happening in those calls. 
No call scoring, no sentiment signals, no visibility into whether agents are resolving issues or contributing to silent member churn.
Their email channel (Outlook) has the same blind spot — roughly 7,000 emails a month with no parsing or pattern detection.

Challenge
We've been trying to get sample call recordings from Choice for two months to build a demo. It hasn't moved — they're navigating PHI/HIPAA concerns and internal bandwidth constraints.

What I've done to unblock this
Rather than keep waiting, I've generated 50 synthetic call scripts written specifically in the context of their business — member calls covering benefits disputes, claim denials, provider issues, pharmacy, billing, enrollment. 
Each script includes an AI insights panel mapped to our 5-layer NanoVox architecture (transcription, call insights, agent scoring, operational BI, real-time assist).

This gives the team real, contextually accurate input data to build and demo the solution without needing actual client recordings.
