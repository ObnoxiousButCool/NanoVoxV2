The pattern behind the numbers

Agents are not getting the facts wrong. Information Accuracy is the second cleanest tenet at 86%, and across the corpus accuracy earned 70 positive markers against 17 negative — a four-to-one ratio. They know the annual maximum, the waiting period, the frequency limit.

What they don't do is finish. Follow-Through is the single largest destroyer of score: 690 points across 51 calls, and it carries a four-to-one ratio in the opposite direction — 69 negative markers against 4 positive. No case number, no callback, referred to the employer, told to call back later.

That distinction changes what you do about it. A knowledge problem is fixed with training. This is a process and accountability problem — agents closing calls they have not resolved, because nothing in the workflow requires them to.
